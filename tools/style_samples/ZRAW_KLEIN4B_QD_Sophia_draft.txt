the diamond queen stands sovereign in a sunlit star library where domes of glass skylight pour golden light over ancient marble stairs etched with celestial symbols, her long red-orange hair cascading like molten fire beneath a radiant diamond tiara that glimmers with inner light; she wears a black and gold veil gown woven from threads of constellations, each star on the fabric sharp as emerald and amber, while scrolls float in midair beside an astrolabe spinning secrets of time; doves drift through shafts of sunbeams filtered through dust motes that dance like lost thoughts, illuminating star charts pinned to walls above her head—each chart a map not just of galaxies but of wisdom itself; she gazes into the depths with eyes like polished obsidian pools, radiating quiet power and endless knowledge, embodying Gnostic Sophia as both divine insight and living mystery.
