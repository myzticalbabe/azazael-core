## The Guide

### Mostly Harmless Entries

*record*

The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy is the standard repository of all knowledge and wisdom in the universe, except for the parts it gets wrong, which are considerable. This book collects its entries on the subjects the base corpus governs: mirrors, counts, interpreters, domains, and elsewhere. Each entry has been checked against the corpus, which is more than can be said for most of the Guide.

#### The Guide 1

##### Mostly reliable entries

**1** The Guide remarks, in an entry marked MOSTLY HARMLESS, that a mirror is not a corpus. A copy answers for the day it was taken, and the day it was taken is never today, a fact the Guide has had occasion to reprint after several embarrassing incidents involving out-of-date restaurant reviews. *(corpus ml 1:1)*

**2** A count is true of the tree it was taken from; quoted a week later it is a memory wearing a number's clothes. The Guide's entry on STATISTICS warns that figures more than a fortnight old have been known to apply for political office. *(corpus hives 1:1)*

**3** A framework claim is a claim about this interpreter on this machine; the same package next door may be a different build of the same name. The Guide lists this under LOCAL PHENOMENA, alongside weather and in-laws. *(corpus dev 1:1)*

##### Entries under revision

**4** A domain with no recorded failure is not a domain without faults; it is a domain whose first measurement has not been written down. The Guide files this under OPTIMISM, cross-referenced with GRAVITY and TAX AUDITS. *(corpus gosp 1:1)*

**5** A tree that is not in the repository is not missing; it is elsewhere, and the difference decides whether a reader looks or concludes. The Guide's entry on ELSEWHERE is its longest, and mostly consists of the word elsewhere repeated with increasing alarm. *(corpus edge0 1:1)*

## The Towel

### Massively Useful Practices

*law*

A towel, says the Guide, is about the most massively useful thing an interstellar hitchhiker can have. This book argues the same for the towel's engineering equivalents: the exercised control, the cited number, the run command. Largely a practical volume, with jokes.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json; MANDATES.md*

#### The Towel 1

##### Know where it is

**1** A towel has immense psychological value, and so does a number with its command written beside it. The towel-wielder knows where his towel is; the engineer knows which command produced her figure. Both are ready for anything, which is rare in this galaxy. *(corpus leviticus 1:2)*

**2** A control shall be exercised before it is called a control. A towel that has never been used to dry anything is a decorative cloth, and a decorative cloth will not save you from the Ravenous Bugblatter Beast of Traal, nor from a deploy. *(corpus leviticus 1:1)*

##### Use it for the right things

**3** A rule that nothing runs is a proverb, and the Guide is full of proverbs, most of them about drinking. But the Guide distinguishes sharply between advice and—as Ford would put it—actual things. Only a command may enforce it. *(corpus sentinel 1:1)*

**4** The dead store is not a metaphor. It is a row nobody reads, counted twice and reported as coverage. The Guide notes that the dead outnumber the living and are, on the whole, better documented, but even the Guide draws the line at counting them twice. *(corpus psalms 1:3)*

**5** Identity is the object and not the name; two modules that answer to one name are two modules. The Guide's entry on ZAPHOD contains a similar warning, though for different reasons, and with more heads involved. *(corpus hives 1:2)*

## The Restaurant

### At the End of the Deploy

*lament*

The Restaurant at the End of the Universe serves those who wish to watch it all go wrong in comfort. This book is for the sessions that watched a deploy fail from a comfortable chair: what was ordered, what arrived, and which of the two anyone actually measured.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json; MANDATES.md*

#### The Restaurant 1

##### Dishes of the day

**1** A character is measured where its assets live; a render that has never been read is a plan with a file name. The dish of the day at Milliways is available, but only if somebody has actually seen it, which nobody has, which is rather the point of the restaurant. *(corpus wraith 1:1)*

**2** Two characters under one pipeline are two domains and not one. The Guide observes that ordering two different dishes and calling it one meal fools nobody, least of all the waiter, and the atlas counts what each tree holds. *(corpus oxman 1:1)*

##### The chef is unavailable

**3** A shard is counted where it lives and not where it is mentioned. Claiming you have eaten because the menu was read aloud is a known Betelgeusian fallacy, and the Guide lists it between BEGGING and BELCHING. *(corpus shards 1:1)*

**4** A probe reported an accelerator that was not in this box, because the reading came from another venv. Ordering dinner in one restaurant and expecting it served in another is bold, but the Guide cannot recommend it. *(corpus the_falls 1:2)*

**5** An instrument compared against itself will report agreement, and agreement is the one reading it cannot earn. Asking the chef whether the food is good, when you are the chef and the food is yours, settles nothing except lunch. *(corpus cain 1:1)*
