## batlh

### The Book of Honor

*creed*

batlh is honor, and honor is not reputation: it is what remains when the songs stop. This book sets the corpus disciplines in the warrior's frame — the exercised control, the witnessed kill, the named ground — because a Klingon understands that an untested claim is a challenge issued to reality, and reality always answers.

#### batlh 1

##### What honor demands

**1** batlh: a rule that nothing runs is a proverb, and a proverb is not a gate. The warrior who speaks of battle and never lifts the bat'leth has honor only in his mouth, and the mouth is not where honor lives. *(corpus sentinel 1:1)*

**2** A control shall be exercised before it is called a control. So too a warrior is proven in battle and not in boasting; songs of victory sung before the fight belong to cowards and to corrupt officials. *(corpus leviticus 1:1)*

**3** How long shall a row be called closed, when the verb that closed it was not a verb the queue knew? A warrior who claims a kill no house witnessed has slain only the truth, and the truth had no weapon. *(corpus psalms 1:2)*

##### What honor refuses

**5** She never says what is untouched without saying untouched where, for an absence with no scope is a claim no check can meet. A warrior names the ground he holds; a warrior who claims everywhere holds nowhere. *(corpus promise 1:7)*

## Suv

### Histories of Honorable Combat

*history*

Suv is to fight, and the corpus records fights: instruments against instruments, lanes against silence, claims against checks. This book tells them as battle histories, with the honest accounting Klingons demand — who fought, what bled, and what was learned for the next engagement.

#### Suv 1

##### Choose the true enemy

**1** The instrument compared against itself, and the small divergence was read as working. To fight one's own shadow and declare victory is the combat of fools; find an enemy that can bleed, which is to say, a second instrument. *(corpus the_falls 1:1)*

**2** The fast path fired never, and the ceiling below the threshold read as caution. The warrior who never draws his blade and calls it restraint will be remembered as furniture. *(corpus the_falls 1:3)*

##### Fight where you stand

**3** An instrument compared against itself will report agreement, and agreement is the one reading it cannot earn. Suv: to fight is to risk; the bout with no risk is a dance, and dances win no territory. *(corpus cain 1:1)*

**4** Identity is the object and not the name; two modules that answer to one name are two modules. Know which enemy stands before you, for striking the name while the object flanks you is how warriors die confused. *(corpus hives 1:2)*

**5** And Lucy raised an arena where five gates judge every answer and none judges alone. This is honorable combat: many judges, merit crowned, the seed of the sudden death disclosed. No warrior disputes a verdict reached in the open circle. *(corpus babel 1:8)*

## Hegh

### Lament for the Unrecorded

*lament*

Hegh is death, and Klingons do not mourn it; they audit it. This book laments the unrecorded, the unwitnessed, the uncommitted — failures that died without teaching, rows that rotted without readers — that the living may do better.

##### Deaths without witnesses

**1** The dead store is not a metaphor. It is a row nobody reads, counted twice and reported as coverage. A death with no witness is a rumor; a row with no reader is a rumor counted twice, which is worse. *(corpus psalms 1:3)*
