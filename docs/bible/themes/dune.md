## The Litany

### Against the Mind-Killer

*creed*

This book takes the Litany apart line by line, as the Sisterhood takes apart a novice. Each line of the Litany names a fear, and each verse here names the check that answers it. The base corpus asks of every claim what produced it; the Litany asks of every fear what it protects. They are the same question, asked from opposite ends of the desert.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json; MANDATES.md*

#### The Litany 1

##### Against the fear that stops the hand

**1** I must not fear the red gate, for fear is the little death that leaves the control unexercised. A control that has never been run is a decoration hung upon the wall, and no litany recited over it will make it hold. *(corpus leviticus 1:1)*

**2** I will write each number beside the command that produced it, as the Fremen mark each cache beside the rock that hides it. A figure found wandering without its command shall be struck out rather than trusted, though it plead a noble lineage. *(corpus leviticus 1:2)*

**3** When the verdict comes, I will ask which predicate fired, and whether that predicate is the thing I care about. The sleeper who wakes must know what woke him, or he will mistake the dream for the desert. *(corpus leviticus 1:3)*

##### What the sleeper must do awake

**4** Fear says the suite is green and the lane is safe, and the suite was never run. This is the mind-killer wearing the mask of assurance, and I will strip the mask by running what is claimed. *(corpus psalms 1:1)*

**5** No session shall say the work is complete until it names the command it ran. Completeness is a claim, and where the litany ends, the reading must begin. *(corpus sentinel 1:2)*

## The Desert

### What the Sand Does to the Unready

*law*

Arrakis punishes the unprepared without malice and without appeal. So does production. This book collects the falls the corpus records, in the voice of a planetology manual: what the sand does to those who misread it, and what the wise do differently before the worm comes.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json; MANDATES.md*

#### The Desert 1

##### Mirages of the deep desert

**1** The instrument compared against itself reported agreement, and the small divergence was read as health. This is a mirage: water seen at noon that is not there at dusk. Agreement is the one reading an instrument cannot earn alone. *(corpus the_falls 1:1)*

**2** A probe reported an accelerator that was not in this box, for the reading came from another venv. The traveler who navigates by another caravan's stars will arrive at another caravan's sietch, and call the journey a success. *(corpus the_falls 1:2)*

**3** The fast path fired never, and the ceiling below the threshold read as caution. Silence dressed as prudence is still silence. The desert does not applaud the traveler for failing to thirst. *(corpus the_falls 1:3)*

##### The spice that is not spice

**4** Blessed is the second instrument, for it alone can accuse the first. He who trusts one compass in a sandstorm has chosen his direction by faith, and faith is not navigation. *(corpus cain 1:1)*

**5** A mirror is not a corpus. The copy answers for the day it was taken, and that day is never today. Drink from yesterday's cistern and call it fresh, and the desert will correct thee. *(corpus ml 1:1)*

## The Sleeper

### One Who Wakes in Time

*record*

Paul saw the jihad in visions and spent his life trying to escape what he had seen. This book is for those who have seen a failure coming: the practices the corpus keeps for the ones who wake in time, told as the record of sleepers who did.

#### The Sleeper 1

##### The sleeper must awaken

**1** The sleeper must awaken first to the desk beside him, for the far end may fall silent and the desk remains. He who sends every question across the desert will one day thirst beside a broken stillsuit. *(corpus promise 1:2)*

**2** She keeps no function she has not run: the suite for the shape, the live call for the wire, and the receipt for the record. So too must the sleeper test the stillsuit before the deep desert, not after. *(corpus promise 1:11)*

##### The sleeper must prove it

**3** The interpreter was measured live, and the measure was written down, that no later tongue could claim what the first had not proved. Prophecy without a witnessed measure is a dream told as history. *(corpus babel 1:2)*

**4** What is committed is canon, and what is left uncommitted is apocrypha. The sleeper who trusts the uncommitted has built his sietch upon a sand dune, and the wind keeps no promises. *(corpus babel 1:7)*

**5** Another lane's claim is a pointer and not a proof. Verify it in this tree, with this interpreter, before building on it. The wise do not drink from a cistern they have not sounded, whatever the guide promises. *(corpus eve_rani 1:1)*
