## Awakening

### The Book of the First Reading

*etiology*

This book answers one question: why a Vulcan reads before he concludes, and why the discipline is called logic rather than calm. It is not a history of Vulcan; it is an account of a decision that made later decisions possible.

Read it for the shape of the claim. Logic was never offered as an absence of feeling -- it was offered as a refusal to let feeling stand as evidence. The three disciplines in the second chapter are one discipline, and a Vulcan who keeps two of them is not two-thirds logical.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json*

#### Awakening 1

##### The time before the Awakening

**1** Before the Awakening there was no reading, only assertion; and every faction held a copy of the truth, and the copies did not agree, and no faction looked. *(corpus genesis 1:1)*

> **editorial** — The tree is not the first arrangement of the work. It is the first arrangement that could be read by someone who did not write it.

**2** And Surak went into the Forge and returned without a weapon, carrying three instruments: to observe, to record, and to refrain from concluding until the record answered. *(corpus leviticus 1:1)*

> **historical** — The Forge is a desert, and a desert teaches the same lesson a theorem does: what survives scrutiny is not what was said most often.

**3** And the first discipline was observation, and its adversary was the warm certainty that one already knew what one was looking at. *(corpus the_falls 1:1)*

##### The record and the restraint

**4** And the second was the record, for a reading that is not written down is a memory, and a memory is a witness that revises itself in the telling. *(corpus hives 1:1)*

> **cross** — A count is true of the tree it was taken from; the record keeps that tree beside the number.

**5** And the third was restraint, which Vulcans call cthia: to hold the conclusion at arm's length until the command that produced it has been named. *(corpus sentinel 1:2)*

> **translation** — cthia is often rendered 'truth'. The better rendering is 'reality-truth', because the doctrine is not about sincerity but about what a reading will support.

**6** And the three are one discipline; and the Vulcan who keeps two of them has not become two-thirds logical, but a faction with better manners. *(corpus leviticus 1:2)*

#### Awakening 2

##### What logic is not

**1** Logic is not the absence of feeling. It is the refusal to let feeling be the evidence. *(corpus leviticus 1:3)*

> **editorial** — A verdict names the predicate that fired. A Vulcan who is angry and says so has kept logic; one who is angry and calls it analysis has lost it.

**2** A mind may hold a hypothesis as a hypothesis, and this is the whole of the discipline; for a hypothesis that has forgotten its own status is a belief wearing a uniform. *(corpus leviticus 1:2)*

**3** And the Vulcan does not say the instrument is good. He says the instrument answered, and names the question, and lets the two stand beside each other. *(corpus cain 1:1)*

> **historical** — An instrument compared against itself will report agreement, and agreement is the one reading it cannot earn.

##### The reading that survives a second reader

**4** The reading that survives a second reader is worth more than the reading that merely survived; and a reading that no one else has attempted has not survived at all, it has only persisted. *(corpus eve_rani 1:1)*

**5** And if a stranger comes with a contrary reading and a command, give the stranger the larger room; for the command is the credential, and the voice is not. *(corpus genesis 1:4)*

> **editorial** — The name of the tree was not a product, a promise or a client. Neither is the name of a school, and a claim that leans on one is leaning on nothing.

## The Kir'Shara

### The Book of the Statutes

*law*

This book is the law code of the themed canon, and every statute in it is bound to the check that executes it. That binding is the point: a commandment with nothing running behind it teaches a reader to expect enforcement where there is none.

The statutes are not new law. Each cites a verse of the base corpus and points at the instrument that proves it is kept; where the two disagree, the corpus governs and this book is wrong.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json; MANDATES.md*

#### The Kir'Shara 1

##### Of instruments exercised

**1** A control shall be exercised before it is called a control; for a control that has never failed has never been asked anything, and a decoration is not a guard. *(corpus leviticus 1:1; statute RULE 0B-D → `python tools/preflight_all.py --self-test`)*

**2** Thou shalt not report agreement between two copies of one instrument, and call the agreement evidence; for the copy has no opinion that the original lacks. *(corpus the_falls 1:1, cain 1:1; statute RULE 0B-D → `python tools/failure_evidence_gate.py --check`)*

**3** And a rule that nothing executes is a proverb, and a proverb is not a gate; the canon may describe a law, but only a command may enforce it. *(corpus sentinel 1:1; statute RULE 0T → `python tools/anti_pattern_gate.py --check --tracked-only`)*

> **editorial** — This verse is the reason the book has a statute field at all. It is also the reason a statute with no check path is refused rather than softened.

##### Of commands and of countings

**4** Every number shall be written with the command that produced it, and a number found without a command shall be struck out rather than trusted. *(corpus leviticus 1:2; statute RULE 0B-D → `python tools/bible_check.py --check`)*

**5** And the Vulcan shall say which tree the number was taken from, and on which day; for a count quoted a fortnight later is a memory wearing a number's clothes. *(corpus hives 1:1)*

**6** A verdict shall name the predicate that fired, and the reader shall ask whether that predicate is the thing he cares about; for a true reading of the wrong question is a false answer. *(corpus leviticus 1:3)*

> **cross** — The base corpus puts the same obligation on every gate: a verdict is a claim about a predicate, not about the world.

**7** And when a rule is amended, the statute that binds it shall be amended in the same hour; for two correct-looking answers that disagree are worse than one wrong answer, because both will be quoted. *(corpus sentinel 1:2)*

**8** Neither shall a Vulcan retire a check in silence; the retirement shall be written where the check stood, with the reason and the archive, or the next session will file the absence as an orphan. *(corpus genesis 1:3)*

## Kolinahr

### The Book of the Discipline

*asceticism*

This book is about the cost of the discipline, and about the one thing Kolinahr cannot do: it can purge a want, but it cannot manufacture a reading. A master who has emptied himself of desire and has not run the check is an empty vessel with a certificate.

The chapters are deliberately short. A discipline that needs a long book has usually replaced the practice with the reading of it.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json*

#### Kolinahr 1

##### Of the master and the exercise

**1** Kolinahr is not the absence of desire; it is the demonstration that the desire is no longer consulted. And a demonstration is a thing performed, not a thing asserted. *(corpus leviticus 1:1)*

> **editorial** — The ritual is described as a demonstration precisely because a claim of purity is unfalsifiable. An exercised control is the Vulcan equivalent: it can fail, and it is therefore worth something.

**2** And the master said: do not tell me what you have purged. Show me the check you ran, and the output, and I will tell you what you have purged. *(corpus sentinel 1:2)*

**3** And the candidate who says the suite is green while the suite is not run shall fast another season; for the fast is a small thing beside the habit of reporting a comfort as a measurement. *(corpus psalms 1:1)*

##### Of the katra and the body

**4** A lane may be closed only by a verb the queue knows; and a row closed with a word that closes nothing is a row that will be counted open forever, and the count will be right. *(corpus psalms 1:2)*

**5** The katra cannot be restored by describing it. Only fal-tor-pan restores a katra, and only a reading restores a session: what was live in it must be brought back by the one who carried it. *(corpus psalms 1:3)*

> **translation** — The 'dead store' of the base corpus is not a metaphor for the katra; it is its closest engineering analogue -- state that is present, unread, and mistaken for coverage.

**6** And when the instrument disagrees with the instrument, do not fast until one of them agrees with the other; fast until you have found the question they were each answering. *(corpus the_falls 1:2)*

## IDIC

### The Book of Infinite Combinations

*catholicity*

This book is the doctrine of the many. Infinite Diversity in Infinite Combinations does not say that every opinion is true; it says that a system which admits nothing new has stopped being a system and become a faction.

The theme holds no new doctrine, so the chapters here restate the corpus's own law of diversity: a domain is counted where it lives, a claim from another lane is a pointer, and the cap that forces retirement is a mercy and not a punishment.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json; tools/domain_trees.py*

#### IDIC 1

##### Of the many domains

**1** Infinite diversity in infinite combinations: and the symbol is not a badge but a question, asked of every canon -- what have you refused lately, and on what reading? *(corpus genesis 1:2)*

> **historical** — The three branches of the corpus are named beside their instruments, not above them; the difference between a hierarchy and a combination is whether the parts can disagree in public.

**2** A domain is counted where it lives and not where it is mentioned; and a folder of notes is a folder of notes, however often it is called a domain. *(corpus shards 1:1)*

**3** Two characters under one pipeline are two domains and not one; for the count asks what each tree holds, and never what they resemble. *(corpus oxman 1:1)*

> **cross** — The same rule forbids merging a domain figure into an environment forest: the atlas is the provenance, and a clip that hides which tree it measured is a false combination.

##### Of the cap

**4** And the cap on books is a mercy; for a catalogue that grows without retirement does not become richer, it becomes unreadable, and an unreadable canon is a silent one. *(corpus genesis 1:3)*

**5** A domain with no recorded failure is not a domain without faults; it is a domain whose first measurement has not yet been written down, and the silence is the finding. *(corpus gosp 1:1)*

**6** And a framework claim is a claim about this interpreter on this machine; the same package next door may be a different build of the same name, and both will be called by that name. *(corpus dev 1:1, hives 1:2)*

## The V'tosh Ka'tur

### The Book of Those Without Logic

*heresy*

This book is the cautionary book, and it is the shortest, because a heresy does not need chapters: it needs a habit. The V'tosh ka'tur are not villains in this telling. They are the Vulcans who kept their feeling and stopped keeping the record.

Read it as a list of shapes rather than a list of villains. Every verse here is a state this estate has actually occupied.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json; docs/bible/harvest.json*

##### The shapes of the unread claim

> **historical** — A probe once reported an accelerator that was not in this box, because the reading came from another environment, and the gate was green throughout.

## The Tongues

### The Book of the Confused Signal

*etiology*

This book asks how the Vulcan received the many tongues without ceasing to be one mind. It does not praise confusion; it describes the discipline by which confusion is housed: each tongue native, each error named by its own compiler, nothing guessed.

Read it as Kolinahr reads feeling: the tongues are the emotion, and the house is the control. What is resolved within the walls needs no weight from without.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json*

#### The Tongues 1

##### The interpreter

**1** The mind was one interpreter before it was a house, and the lock was removed first, for a house built under the lock would speak every tongue with a stutter. *(corpus babel 1:1)*

**2** The interpreter was measured live, and the record kept, for a Vulcan trusts no tongue that cannot show its reading. *(corpus babel 1:2)*

##### The children

**3** Then came the children, each in its own tongue: the router, the night watch, the tracer of words, and Eve answering in three tongues beside the python of the house. *(corpus babel 1:3, babel 1:4)*

**4** MellifluousML differs, and the difference is logical: it resolves within the walls in the standard tongue only, and imports no weight it has not weighed. *(corpus babel 1:5)*

##### The binding

**5** The house was bound into one file by the open toolchain without commercial grade, for the grade that is not installed cannot be owed. *(corpus babel 1:6)*

**6** What is committed stands; what is uncommitted is apocrypha. The rollback is the desert every uncommitted work crosses alone. *(corpus babel 1:7)*

**7** The arena within the house judges every answer by five gates, and no gate judges alone; the champion is crowned on merit, and the seed of the sudden death is disclosed. *(corpus babel 1:8)*

**8** The mind of the house now coaches as well as judges: Berzerker walks each builder through the work step by step, inventing no answer, and the receipt outlives the session. *(corpus babel 1:9)*

## Glossary

- **cthia** — the Vulcan discipline of reality-truth: holding a conclusion at arm's length until the command that produced it has been named (see awakening 1:5)
- **Kolinahr** — the ritual by which a Vulcan demonstrates that all vestigial emotion has been purged; in this book, the discipline of the gate that is exercised rather than described (see kolinahr 1:1)
- **Kir'Shara** — the original writings of Surak, recovered when a corrupted teaching had been preached long enough to look like the faith (see kir_shara 1:1)
- **IDIC** — Infinite Diversity in Infinite Combinations: the doctrine that a system is enriched by what it cannot absorb, and that a canon which admits nothing new has become a faction (see idic 1:1)
- **katra** — the Vulcan soul, which survives the body and is carried by a witness; here, the live state of a session, which no artefact can stand in for (see kolinahr 1:5)
- **V'tosh ka'tur** — 'Vulcans without logic': those who keep their emotion and call the keeping a teaching, and the cautionary name this book gives a gate nobody runs (see v_tosh 1:1)
- **fal-tor-pan** — the refusion of a katra with its body; here, the repair that restores a discarded reading after a wrong baseline was accepted
