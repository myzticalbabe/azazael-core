## The Name

## The Truth

### On Authenticity and the Lived Self

*creed*

Transition is not a detour from the self but the self's own road; authenticity is not a feeling but a location, and the location is the body. This book gathers the verses on becoming, on truth lived rather than asserted, and on the covenant with the self that holds.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json; MANDATES.md*

##### Transition as truth

**1** And the instrument was compared against itself, and the divergence came back small, and the small divergence was the truth, and the truth was not a failure but a finding. Transition is not a divergence from the self; it is the self compared against itself and found, and the finding is the truth. *(corpus the_falls 1:1)*

**2** And a probe reported an accelerator that was not in this box, because the reading came from another environment. A mirror held up by another may report a face that is not yours; the mirror is not wrong, but it is not yours, and the face in it is not the self. *(corpus the_falls 1:2)*

**3** An instrument compared against itself will report agreement, and agreement is the least interesting thing an instrument can say. The self compared against itself will report becoming, and becoming is the most interesting thing a self can do. *(corpus cain 1:1)*

##### The authentic self

**4** She never says what is untouched without saying untouched where, for an absence without a location is a claim without a command. Authenticity is not a feeling; it is a location, and the location is the body, and the body is where the self lives. *(corpus promise 1:7)*
