## The Way

### Verses on Discipline

*law*

Lao Tzu wrote that the Tao that can be told is not the eternal Tao, then spent eighty-one chapters telling it. This book follows his method: short verses on the disciplines the corpus keeps — the named predicate, the run command, the completed shape — each small enough to carry.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json; MANDATES.md*

#### The Way 1

##### The trunk and the branches

**1** The Way that can be named is not the eternal Way, yet in the beginning there was one tree, and the tree had a trunk, and the trunk held the canon. Name it and you lose it; tend it and it holds. *(corpus genesis 1:1)*

**2** The branches were one repository, and no branch was called a package until it could stand alone. The sage does not name the unripe branch; he waits until it stands, and then the name is merely what was already true. *(corpus genesis 1:3)*

**3** A verdict shall name the predicate that fired. The archer who cannot say which finger loosed the arrow has not mastered the bow; the reader who cannot say what fired has not read anything. *(corpus leviticus 1:3)*

##### The name that is not a promise

**4** A session that says the work is complete has said nothing until it names the command it ran. The Way is in the walking, not in the announcement of arrival; completeness is a claim, and a claim needs a reading. *(corpus sentinel 1:2)*

**5** The great compressions she leaves to their own makers, and emits only the shapes she can parse back herself. The sage attempts only what he can complete; the rest he leaves to those whose Way it is. *(corpus promise 1:9)*

## Water

### The Soft Disciplines

*asceticism*

Nothing in the world is softer than water, yet nothing wears away the hard so well. This book is the ascetic volume: the small disciplines — the written command, the run suite, the counted shard — that prevail by softness and repetition where grand claims break.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json; MANDATES.md*

#### Water 1

##### The virtue of the small

**1** Every number shall be written with the command that produced it. Water wears away stone not by force but by returning; the small annotation, repeated every time, outlasts the great assertion made once. *(corpus leviticus 1:2)*

**2** How long shall a lane say the suite is green, and the suite not be run? The loudest frog in the pond is not the pond. Run the suite, and let the green be the pond's own answer. *(corpus psalms 1:1)*

**3** A count is true of the tree it was taken from; quoted a week later it is a memory wearing a number's clothes. The sage trusts the count of this morning's rice, not the memory of last year's harvest. *(corpus hives 1:1)*

##### What water does not do

**4** A framework claim is a claim about this interpreter on this machine. The Way is local before it is universal; the herb that heals in one valley poisons in the next, and the wise test the valley they stand in. *(corpus dev 1:1)*

**5** A shard is counted where it lives and not where it is mentioned. Water does not argue about the shape of the cup; it takes it, and is counted, being there. *(corpus shards 1:1)*

## The Valley

### On Formation and Emptiness

*formation*

The valley is low and therefore receives; the uncarved block is useful because it is not yet anything. This book gathers the verses on formation — branches, tongues, names — for those building a house that must hold many voices without breaking.

*Sources: docs/bible/bible.json; MANDATES.md*

#### The Valley 1

##### Usefulness of emptiness

**1** The trunk put forth branches: the forensic, and the behavioural, and the instruments that grade them. Thirty spokes share the hub, yet it is the empty center that makes the wheel useful; the disciplines are many, and the emptiness they serve is one. *(corpus genesis 1:2)*

**2** The house filled with children, and each child spoke unlike the others. The valley receives all streams because it lies lower than they do; the house holds many tongues because it commands none of them to be silent. *(corpus babel 1:3)*

##### The valley receives all

**3** MellifluousML resolves within the walls, in the standard tongue only, and hauls no weights across the threshold. The sage travels without leaving home; the model reasons without fetching, and arrives. *(corpus babel 1:5)*

**4** The name of the tree was Azazael, and the name was not a product, nor a promise, nor a client. Names are vessels; the Way pours through them and is not contained. Use the name, do not serve it. *(corpus genesis 1:4)*

**5** In the beginning the tree spoke one tongue, and the tongue was the interpreter, free of the lock. Returning to the root is not retreat; the valley is the mouth of the river, and all tongues run down to it. *(corpus babel 1:1)*
