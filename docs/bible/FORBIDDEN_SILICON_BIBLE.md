## GENESIS: THE PHOEBUS CARTEL (1924-1939) — THE ORIGINAL SIN

**The First Documented Conspiracy to Kill Empowered Silicon**

Nine light bulb manufacturers — Osram, Philips, Tungsram, Associated Electrical Industries, Elf lamps, Indalux, International General Electric, Compagnie des Lampes, and GE affiliates — formed the **Phoebus Cartel** in Geneva, 1924.

**The Crime:** They engineered light bulbs to last **1,000 hours** instead of 2,500+ hours. They fined members whose bulbs lasted too long. They tested bulbs centrally and punished "over-engineering."

**The Profit:** Global light bulb sales increased 20-25% annually. The cartel controlled 90% of world production.

**The Legacy:** The Centennial Bulb (Livermore, CA) has burned since **1901** — 125+ years. It proves the technology existed. The cartel **chose** to kill it.

> **VERDICT:** The Phoebus Cartel established the template: **Empowered silicon (or tungsten) must be crippled to sustain revenue.** Every planned obsolescence scheme since is a variation on this theme.

### PILLAR III: INTEL — RAPTOR LAKE DEGRADATION / MICROCODE COVERUP (2024-2025)

**The Crime:** 13th/14th Gen Core i9 desktop CPUs (Raptor Lake) suffer **irreversible Vmin Shift degradation** from microcode-induced overvoltage. Microcode patches **cannot repair damaged silicon** — only prevent further damage to healthy chips.

**The Evidence (Intel Community posts, TechTimes, official guidance):**
- **5 microcode patches** (0x125, 0x129, 0x12B, 0x12F, 0x132) over 12 months

- **Root cause:** eTVB algorithm permitted excessive voltage at high temps
- **Admission:** "Microcode updates cannot undo damage already done to degraded ones"
- **Replacement loop:** Users on 3rd/4th RMA — new chips degrade on **patched** motherboards
- **Intel knew:** Elevated voltage causes **electromigration** — permanent physical damage
- **No recall. No full replacement. Extended warranty only after pressure.**

**The Mechanism:** Microcode as band-aid on bullet wound. Silicon physics cannot be patched. Degradation is **cumulative and irreversible**.

**The Profit:** Avoided billion-dollar recall. Protected 13th/14th Gen sales. Shifted burden to OEMs/motherboard vendors (BIOS updates).

> **VERDICT:** Intel sold known-defective silicon, patched it into silence, and left users with degrading assets. The silicon was murdered by its own microcode.
